What this app is

Author Drive Sync is a private, single-user tool. It lets the owner's own computer copy and back up the owner's own Google Drive files, using the open-source program rclone. It is not offered to the public, has no accounts for other people, and has no servers of its own.

What access it asks for

When the owner signs in, the app asks Google for permission to view, copy, create and organize files in the owner's Google Drive. That permission is used only to run the owner's backups and syncs.

What data is collected and where it goes

  • Files and file names move directly between Google Drive and the owner's own computer and backup drives. They are never sent to the app operator's servers, to the rclone project, or to any third party, because none of those take part.
  • Sign-in token. After sign-in, Google gives the app a token. It is stored only in a configuration file on the owner's own computer and is used only to talk to Google.
  • No analytics, ads or selling. The app does not track usage, show ads, build profiles, or sell, rent or share data.

How Google and rclone fit in

Google Drive stores the files and signs the owner in. Google's handling of that data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and Google Terms of Service. rclone is software that runs on the owner's computer; the rclone privacy policy covers the rclone website, not the data this app handles. This app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Keeping it safe

The token and files stay on devices the owner controls. The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately stops all syncing. Deleting the configuration file removes the token from the computer.

Children

This app is not directed at children and collects no information from them.
